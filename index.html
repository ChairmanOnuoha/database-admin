About

I am a SQL Server DBA with more than four years of experience supporting database applications. My work centers on backup and recovery, database security, high availability, performance tuning, index maintenance and disaster recovery.

I have worked in development and production environments on SQL Server 2005 through 2022, in 24x7 operations, on standalone servers and on clusters. I like clear runbooks, monitoring that catches problems early and backup strategies that are tested against the service level the business needs.

Since August 2025 I have also been an instructor at NSTAR Technologies in Atlanta, where I set the curriculum for the DBA program.
